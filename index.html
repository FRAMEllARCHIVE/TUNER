How to Use

1. Click the "Start Tuning" button and allow microphone access.

2. Play one string at a time on your guitar.

3. Watch the note display and tuning indicator to see if your string is in tune.

4. Adjust your string until the indicator centers and the note matches your target.

5. Select different strings from the buttons above to tune your entire guitar.
